Parrocchettami vs FluidVoice.

Two local apps, but with different workflows: transcribe an audio file or dictate directly where you are writing.

The difference in one line

Parrocchettami starts with an audio file or a ready recording. FluidVoice is designed for speaking and making text appear directly in other apps.

Which should you choose?

Choose Parrocchettami if...

  • You have interviews, voice notes, lessons, or recordings to transcribe.
  • You want to work without an account or uploads.
  • You need plain text, timestamps, or SRT subtitles.

Choose FluidVoice if...

  • You want to speak while writing in Slack, Notion, an editor, or other apps.
  • You want a shortcut for starting dictation quickly.
  • You want voice typing that works like a keyboard input.

In short

FluidVoice is probably better for dictating on the go. Parrocchettami is simpler when you start with audio and want a transcript to review, edit, or export.
