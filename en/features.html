Features

Two ways to get to text.

Choose an existing audio file or record directly from your Mac's microphone. Everything else happens locally.

Transcribe or record

Audio transcription

Drop a file into the app or select it from the system file picker. Supports WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, AIFF, and AAC.

Live recording

Record from your Mac's microphone, pause, resume, and start transcription when the recording is ready.

Export that fits your workflow

Rich Text

For reviewing, editing, and copying the transcript.

Timestamp

For keeping the time references for each phrase.

SRT

For creating subtitles compatible with major video tools.

Local history

Reopen recent transcripts without uploading your history to an account.

25 European languages

Parrocchettami uses NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 through parakeet.cpp and lets you choose a language or use automatic detection.

Available languages include Italian, English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Czech, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Hungarian, Romanian, Slovak, Bulgarian, Greek, Croatian, Slovenian, Estonian, Lithuanian, Latvian, and Maltese.
