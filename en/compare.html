When to choose Parrocchettami

  • You want audio to stay on your Mac and not be uploaded to an external service.
  • You use an Apple Silicon Mac
  • You want a simple, free, open source app without a long setup.

When to choose an online service

Otter.ai and Descript may be a better fit if you need collaboration, shared workspaces, or cloud editing features. The trade-off is that audio must be sent to the service and you depend on its account and plan.

When to use Whisper

Whisper remains a good alternative. Parrocchettami is designed for people who want strong performance for supported European languages with modest resource use.
