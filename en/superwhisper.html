Parrocchettami vs Superwhisper.

Parrocchettami is a Mac app focused on transcription. Superwhisper also covers dictation, custom modes, and text transformation.

The difference in one line

Parrocchettami keeps the workflow short: audio, transcription, export. Superwhisper adds a broader system for dictating, formatting, and adapting text to different contexts.

Which should you choose?

Choose Parrocchettami if...

  • You want a simple app for transcribing recordings.
  • Privacy and offline use matter more than extra features.
  • You need TXT, timestamp, and SRT export without an account.

Choose Superwhisper if...

  • You want to dictate in any app with shortcuts.
  • You need modes for email, notes, meetings, or messages.
  • You want to choose between local and cloud models with custom instructions.

In short

Superwhisper is more complete and flexible if you want to use voice as a general input method. Parrocchettami is smaller and more direct: it transcribes audio on your Mac and leaves you with text to review or export.
