Privacy by design

Transcription stays on your Mac.

Parrocchettami turns audio into text without sending files to external services.

What happens to your data

1. Audio

You can drop an audio file into the app or record from your Mac's microphone.

2. Transcribe locally

The file is converted and processed by the NVIDIA Parakeet model running locally on your Mac.

3. Export the text

You can get formatted text, timestamped text, or SRT subtitles.

4. Delete the audio

Audio is never uploaded to external services and is automatically deleted after transcription.

Does it really work offline?

Yes, after the initial AI model download. A connection is needed for the model and updates, not for everyday transcription.

No account

Parrocchettami does not require registration, login, or a cloud account to transcribe your audio.

What is stored

Transcript history is kept locally in the app archive so you can reopen results. Audio files are deleted after transcription.

Code verificabile

The project is open source. You can read the code on GitHub and build the app directly from source.

Model note: the AI model is about 707 MB and is downloaded separately from the app. After downloading, the model stays on your Mac.
